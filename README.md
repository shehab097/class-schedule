**Class Schedule** is a smart, efficient, and user-friendly Android application designed to help students, parents, and teachers organize study times, track lectures and classes, and seamlessly coordinate schedules. 

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **📅 Comprehensive Schedule Management**: Easily add, edit, and organize courses, lectures, classrooms, start/end times, and days of the week.
- **👥 Student & Class Management**: Create student or class profiles and generate unique ID codes to easily share schedules.
- **👨‍🏫 Teacher & Professor Tracking**: Add instructor accounts and share codes to follow specific lecture and class timetables.
- **🔗 Schedule Importing & Linking**: Enter student or teacher codes from other accounts to instantly import and track schedules in one centralized place.
- **🔔 Smart Notification System**: Get accurate, timely notifications before lessons begin (e.g., 15–30 minutes prior) or at exact start times, with customizable notification sources.
- **⏳ Live Class Timers**: Real-time countdowns for ongoing (live) lectures and upcoming classes, complete with break and interval tracking.
- **🔍 Smart Daily Filtering & Grouping**: Group your daily agenda instantly by student, group, or instructor.
- **🌐 Bilingual Support**: Full support for both **English** and **Arabic** with automatic RTL/LTR layout orientation switching.
- **🌙 Dark Mode Support**: Eye-friendly light and dark themes with customizable color coding for different subjects and students.
- **🔒 Privacy & Security**: Robust data protection and secure account handling.
